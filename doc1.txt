1. Project Overview
The AI Seller Management Platform is designed for sellers who operate across marketplaces such as
Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra while also selling through an offline clothing store or hand-cart. The
platform provides one central system for products, inventory, orders, pricing, offline billing and
AI-assisted marketplace listing.
The key idea is to maintain a Master Product Catalog. A seller creates one product in the platform, AI
prepares the content and marketplace-specific mappings are generated, and supported marketplace
integrations publish or synchronize the listing.
The system should not store marketplace passwords or OTPs. Marketplace authentication should use
each marketplace's supported authorization/API mechanism. The seller completes login/OTP/2FA on
the marketplace's official page when required; the backend securely manages the resulting authorized
credentials/tokens.
Primary business domain: Fashion & Clothing, including products sold online and offline from a hand-cart/store.
2. Main Objectives
●
Connect seller accounts to supported marketplaces through official authorization/integration
mechanisms.
●
Create and manage a single master catalog with SKU, variants, price, cost and inventory.
●
Use AI to generate product titles, descriptions, categories, attributes, keywords and listing-ready
content from product images and seller inputs.
●
Map master product attributes to marketplace-specific category and attribute requirements.
●
Publish products to supported marketplaces after seller review and approval.
●
Synchronize orders, inventory, prices and returns where marketplace APIs support those operations.
●
Maintain offline products and offline sales even when a product is not listed online.
●
Provide a unified inventory so online and offline sales affect the same stock position.
●
Provide fashion trend intelligence such as trending categories, styles and colors using verifiable data
sources/signals.
●
Provide dashboards, reports, alerts and business analytics.

15. Fashion Trend Intelligence
The Trend Intelligence module should use current and historical signals from permitted/reliable
sources. AI can summarize and interpret those signals, but the system should distinguish measured
data from AI-generated inference.
●
Trending product categories: oversized T-shirts, cargo pants, baggy jeans, etc.
●
Trending colors and styles.
●
Trend direction: Rising, Stable or Declining.
●
Time-window comparisons: 7 days, 30 days and custom periods.
●
Seller's own sales trend compared with broader market signals.
●
AI-generated product opportunities based on available data.
●
Confidence/source indicators so the seller can understand where a trend insight came from.

18. Security Requirements
●
Use HTTPS everywhere in production.
●
Use Firebase ID token verification on protected backend routes.
●
Never store marketplace passwords or OTPs.
●
Encrypt sensitive marketplace tokens/credentials at rest where applicable.
●
Keep API keys and secrets in environment variables or a secrets manager.
●
Use rate limiting, request validation and centralized error handling.
●
Implement role-based access if staff accounts are added.
●
Maintain audit logs for marketplace connection, product publication, price changes and inventory
adjustments.
