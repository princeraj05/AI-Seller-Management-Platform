1. Project Overview
The AI Seller Management Platform is a centralized commerce management system for sellers who operate across
marketplaces, their own ecommerce website, and offline stores. The seller creates or imports a product once; AI
assists with product content and channel-specific information, while the platform manages publishing, inventory,
orders, returns, pricing, analytics, and trend intelligence from one dashboard.
The architecture uses a Master Product as the single source of product truth. Marketplace and website integrations
act as channels connected to that master catalog.
2. Main Objectives
• Connect Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra accounts through supported official authorization/integration mechanisms.
• Connect a seller's own ecommerce website through supported platform APIs or a custom integration API.
• Upload product images/details once and use AI to generate titles, descriptions, categories, attributes, keywords and
SEO content.
• Publish the approved master product to multiple sales channels.
• Maintain centralized inventory across marketplaces, own website and offline POS.
• Synchronize orders, returns, prices and stock wherever the connected channel APIs permit.
• Provide fashion and commerce trend intelligence such as rising categories, colors and styles.
• Keep credentials, tokens, permissions and audit events secure.

4. Own Website Integration
A seller may already have an ecommerce website such as Shopify, WooCommerce, Wix, or a custom-built store. The
platform should treat the website as another sales channel rather than as a separate product database.

19. Security Requirements
• Use HTTPS for every integration and API request.
• Verify Firebase ID tokens on protected backend routes.
• Never ask sellers to enter or store marketplace OTP/passwords in your application.
• Use official OAuth/authorization/API mechanisms wherever the provider supports them.
• Encrypt sensitive marketplace and website credentials/tokens at rest.
• Keep secrets in environment variables or a secrets manager.
• Use rate limiting, schema validation, RBAC, centralized error handling and audit logs.


--- PAGE 9 ---
• Use idempotency for publishing, inventory synchronization and order ingestion.
• Verify webhook signatures where supported.
• Keep channel-specific permissions as narrow as possible.

21. End-to-End Seller Flow
1. Seller signs in with Firebase
2. Creates store profile
3. Connects Amazon / Flipkart / Myntra
4. Connects own website if available
5. Uploads product image + basic details
6. AI generates product content
7. Seller reviews and approves
8. Master product is saved
9. Channel adapters map the product
10. Product is published to selected channels
11. Orders arrive from marketplaces / website / POS
12. Inventory ledger updates master stock
13. Stock/price changes are synchronized
14. Returns and analytics are consolidated
15. Trend engine provides market insights

23. Development Roadmap
Phase 1 — Foundation: Firebase authentication, seller/store setup, dashboard shell and channel UI.
Phase 2 — Product Core: master product, variants, categories, media and inventory ledger.
Phase 3 — AI: image processing, title/description generation, attributes and review workflow.


--- PAGE 10 ---
Phase 4 — Marketplace Connectors: Amazon, Flipkart and Myntra according to approved APIs/permissions.
Phase 5 — Own Website: Shopify/WooCommerce/Wix/custom adapter, product publishing, order sync and
webhooks.
Phase 6 — Unified Operations: inventory, price sync, returns, POS and notifications.
Phase 7 — Intelligence: trends, analytics, profit reporting and AI recommendations.
Phase 8 — Production Hardening: security audit, idempotency, retries, observability, QA and deployment.
24. Production Design Principles
• Master data first: one canonical product and inventory ledger.
• Adapters isolate provider-specific API differences.
• Event-driven synchronization where webhooks are available.
• Scheduled reconciliation for reliability.
• Every external operation should be retry-safe and idempotent.
• Every important automated action should be observable through logs/audit history.
• AI assists the seller; seller approval remains available before publication.
• Provider API capabilities and permissions must be verified before implementation.

Conclusion
The updated platform is designed as a true omnichannel seller system: marketplaces, the seller's own website, and
offline sales all connect to one master product and inventory system. The new Own Website / Storefront Integration
module allows AI-generated products to be mapped and published to supported ecommerce platforms through their
official APIs or a controlled custom integration, while keeping orders and inventory synchronized back into the central
system.
